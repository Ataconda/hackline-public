HackLine Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

HackLine is a puzzle game for Android and iOS made by Ataconda Games LLC.

HackLine has no account and never asks for your name or email. To improve the game it collects anonymous play statistics and crash reports, and it shows ads from Google AdMob. Both are described below.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your puzzle progress, daily puzzle results and streak, hint use, ad-free purchase, and settings (music, sound effects, haptics, and high contrast) on your phone so you can continue where you left off. Ataconda Games receives only the play statistics described below, not your saved game, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Play statistics and crash reports

HackLine uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services run by Google for Ataconda Games, to learn how the game is played and to fix crashes. The game sends events such as starting, finishing, or leaving a puzzle, the puzzle's number, how long it took, the hints, undos, and stars it used, the time challenge and daily rush scores, whether you watched or skipped the end credits, and changes to settings such as music and sound. It also sends your settings and whether you bought the ad-free unlock. Crash reports contain what the app was doing when it crashed and your device model and operating system version. Firebase identifies your copy of the app with a random ID that is not linked to your name, email, or advertising ID, and Google derives an approximate location from your IP address. Ataconda Games uses this data only to improve HackLine, never sells it, and does not use it for advertising. Individual events are kept for 2 months, and the per-device summary Google Analytics keeps for up to 14 months after the last use.

To stop sending play statistics and crash reports, turn off Settings → Share usage data. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, play statistics are stored only if you agree in the privacy choices form. Google's use of this data is described at Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Ads

HackLine shows a short video when you choose to watch one for a hint or to keep a streak, and a full-screen ad after some time challenge runs. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud, Google collects your device's IP address (which gives an approximate location), advertising identifiers, information about the ads shown and your interactions with them, and app performance and crash information. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On iOS, HackLine shows only non-personalised ads and does not ask to track you. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and some US states, the game asks for your consent before ads load, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's settings.

Removing ads

A one-time purchase removes all ads. Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store; Ataconda Games receives no payment details. Use Settings → Restore purchase to get it back on a new phone.

Daily puzzles

To get new daily puzzles, HackLine downloads puzzle files from GitHub Pages (ataconda.github.io), a hosting service run by GitHub. The game sends nothing about you or your play with these requests. As with any website, GitHub's servers see your device's IP address when it downloads a file, and GitHub may keep it in its logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Ataconda Games does not receive or use this information.

Permissions

On Android, HackLine asks for internet access, to download daily puzzles and ads; vibration, to play haptic feedback; the advertising ID, for ads; and billing, for the ad-free purchase. On iOS, it asks for no permissions.

Reporting an ad

Use Settings → Report an ad, or email the address below.

Children

HackLine is not directed at children under 13.

HackLine Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

HackLine is a puzzle game for Android and iOS made by Ataconda Games.

HackLine collects no personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your puzzle progress, daily puzzle results and streak, hint use, and settings (music, sound effects, haptics, and high contrast) on your phone so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your phone, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Daily puzzles

To get new daily puzzles, HackLine downloads puzzle files from GitHub Pages (ataconda.github.io), a hosting service run by GitHub. The game sends nothing about you or your play with these requests. As with any website, GitHub's servers see your device's IP address when it downloads a file, and GitHub may keep it in its logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Ataconda Games does not receive or use this information. The rest of the game works without an internet connection.

Permissions

On Android, HackLine asks for internet access, to download daily puzzles, and vibration, to play haptic feedback. On iOS, it asks for no permissions.

Children

HackLine is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
